Set Up Call Forwarding

Forward missed calls to your MissedCallio number so Aria answers when you can't.

Your MissedCallio number is shown in your dashboard under Account → Subscription. Replace YOUR_NUMBER below with that number.

Your number: log in to dashboard to see it
AT&T USA
  1. Open your phone dialer.
  2. Dial *71YOUR_NUMBER and press call.
    Example: *71+12025550100
  3. Wait for a confirmation tone or message.
  4. Call forwarding is now active. All unanswered calls go to Aria.
To disable: dial *73 and press call.
Verizon USA
  1. Go to Settings → Phone → Call Forwarding.
  2. Toggle Call Forwarding on.
  3. Enter your MissedCallio number.
  4. Alternatively dial *71YOUR_NUMBER from the dialer.
To disable: dial *73 or toggle off in Settings.
T-Mobile USA
  1. Open your dialer.
  2. Dial **61*YOUR_NUMBER# and press call.
    Forwards when unanswered.
  3. For busy forwarding: dial **67*YOUR_NUMBER#
  4. Confirmation will show on screen.
To disable all forwarding: dial ##002#
EE / O2 / Vodafone UK
  1. EE: Dial **61*YOUR_NUMBER*11*30#
  2. O2: Dial **61*YOUR_NUMBER#
  3. Vodafone: Dial **61*YOUR_NUMBER**30#
  4. Press call and wait for confirmation.
To disable (all UK): dial ##002#
iPhone (any carrier) iOS
  1. Open Settings → Phone.
  2. Tap Call Forwarding.
  3. Toggle it on.
  4. Enter your MissedCallio number.
This forwards ALL calls. For missed-only, use the dialer codes above.
Android (Google Phone app) Android
  1. Open the Phone app → tap the ⋮ menu → Settings.
  2. Tap Calls → Call Forwarding.
  3. Choose Forward when unanswered.
  4. Enter your MissedCallio number and tap Enable.
Steps vary slightly by Android version and manufacturer.
PBX / Office Phone System Business
  1. Log in to your PBX admin panel (e.g. 3CX, RingCentral, Cisco).
  2. Navigate to Extensions → Your Extension → Call Forwarding.
  3. Set No Answer or Overflow to forward to your MissedCallio number.
  4. Save. Test with a call from another line.
For RingCentral: Settings → My Extension → Call Handling & Forwarding.
Common Questions
How do I find my MissedCallio number?

Log in to your dashboard, go to the Account tab, and look under Subscription — your assigned number is listed there as "MissedCallio Number".

Will callers know they've been forwarded?

No. Aria answers naturally as your business receptionist. Callers hear your business name and are greeted warmly — there's no indication the call was forwarded.

Does this affect calls I answer myself?

No — conditional forwarding only kicks in when you don't answer (busy, no answer, or out of range). Calls you pick up are completely unaffected.

My carrier isn't listed. What do I do?

Most carriers worldwide support the standard GSMA call forwarding codes: **61*YOUR_NUMBER# (no answer) and **67*YOUR_NUMBER# (busy). Try these first. If they don't work, contact your carrier's support line and ask to enable "conditional call forwarding to an external number".

I've forwarded but Aria isn't answering. What's wrong?

First check your dashboard — if the Status badge says Expired or Cancelled, Aria has been paused. Otherwise, make sure you used the correct number (with country code). If problems persist, email support@missedcallio.io.
