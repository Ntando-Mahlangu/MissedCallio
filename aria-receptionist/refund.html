Refund Policy

Last updated: October 2026

At MissedCallio, we want you to be completely satisfied with our service. This Refund Policy explains when and how refunds are issued. It forms part of our Terms and Conditions.

Summary: We offer a 3-day free trial — no credit card required. If you experience a verified technical failure on our end within your first 30 days of a paid subscription, we will review your case for a full or partial refund. Outside of these circumstances, subscription payments are non-refundable, except where required by applicable law.

1. Free Trial

All new accounts receive a 3-day free trial with no credit card required. You will not be charged anything during your trial period. If you choose not to continue, simply do not add payment details — your account will be deactivated and you owe nothing.

2. EU and UK Statutory Cooling-Off Right

Important for EU and UK customers: Under the EU Consumer Rights Directive and the UK Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013, consumers ordinarily have a 14-day right to withdraw from digital service contracts without giving a reason. However, this right is waived when you explicitly request that the service begins immediately and acknowledge that you lose your right of withdrawal once the service has been fully performed. By subscribing to MissedCallio and selecting immediate service activation, you acknowledge this waiver. If you have not yet used the service at all after subscribing, you may still be entitled to a full refund within 14 days — contact us to discuss your situation.

3. 30-Day Satisfaction Review

Within your first 30 days of a paid subscription, contact us at support@missedcallio.online and we will review your case in good faith. Refunds or credits within the first 30 days are typically approved when:

  • You experienced a verified technical failure on our end that prevented the service from working as described.
  • You were charged incorrectly due to a billing error on our part.
  • The service was materially different from what was described at the time of purchase.

Refund decisions are made at our reasonable discretion. We will explain our reasoning if a request is declined.

4. No Refunds After 30 Days

After 30 days of a paid subscription, we do not issue refunds for:

  • Partial months — if you cancel mid-cycle, your service continues until the end of the billing period and no partial refund is issued.
  • Unused call allowance — if you did not use all your included calls in a given month.
  • Change of mind or decision not to use the service.
  • Failure to cancel before the next billing cycle.
  • Subscription fees incurred while your account was suspended for Terms violations.

This does not affect any mandatory rights you may have under applicable consumer protection law in your jurisdiction.

5. Technical Failures and Service Credits

If MissedCallio experiences a significant technical outage that prevents Aria from answering calls for more than 24 consecutive hours, you may be eligible for a pro-rated credit applied to your next billing cycle. We will notify affected customers proactively in the event of any major outage. Credits are applied automatically where possible and do not require you to request them.

6. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, email us at support@missedcallio.online with:

  • Your full name and business name
  • The email address associated with your account
  • The reason for your refund request
  • Any relevant details or evidence about the issue you experienced

We aim to respond to all refund requests within 2 business days. Approved refunds are processed within 5–10 business days and returned to your original payment method via Paddle.

7. Cancellation

You may cancel your MissedCallio subscription at any time via your dashboard (Account tab → Subscription → Cancel subscription) or by emailing support@missedcallio.online. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period. You will not be charged again after cancellation, and your access to the service continues until the period end.

8. Chargebacks and Payment Disputes

If you believe you have been charged incorrectly, we encourage you to contact us first at support@missedcallio.online — we resolve genuine billing errors quickly, usually within 2 business days. You always retain the right to raise a dispute with your bank or card provider; however, initiating a chargeback for a legitimate charge may result in suspension of your account pending resolution of the dispute. We cooperate fully with payment provider dispute processes and will provide all relevant documentation.

9. Consumer Protection Rights

Nothing in this Refund Policy is intended to limit or exclude any statutory rights you may have under applicable consumer protection law, including:

  • The Consumer Protection Act 68 of 2008 (South Africa)
  • The Consumer Rights Act 2015 (UK)
  • The EU Consumer Rights Directive 2011/83/EU
  • Any equivalent mandatory consumer protection legislation in your jurisdiction

Where such rights apply and provide greater protection than this policy, those rights prevail.

10. Contact Us

For any refund or billing questions, please reach out to us at:

support@missedcallio.online

We typically respond within 1–2 business days.
