Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

MissedCallio ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and your rights. It applies to missedcallio.online and all related services.

If you are a business using MissedCallio to answer calls, this policy covers both your data (as our customer) and the data collected about your callers on your behalf. You remain the data controller for your callers' data; we act as a data processor on your instructions.

1. Who We Are

MissedCallio is operated as a SaaS business. For questions about this policy or to exercise your rights, contact us at support@missedcallio.online.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account information (you provide)

  • Name, email address, mobile number, and business details provided during signup.
  • Business configuration: hours, address, pricing, staff details, and preferences you enter in your dashboard.

2.2 Caller information (collected by Aria on your behalf)

  • Caller's name, phone number, reason for calling, and any information they voluntarily share with Aria.
  • Call recordings and transcripts: Aria records and transcribes calls to generate lead summaries and voicemail notifications. Recordings are associated with your account and stored securely.
  • Appointment details (name, service, date/time) when callers book through Aria.

2.3 Usage and technical data

  • Call logs (duration, timestamps, outcomes).
  • Dashboard usage — pages visited, features used — to help us improve the service.
  • Session tokens stored in your browser's localStorage to keep you logged in.
  • IP addresses and device type collected by our infrastructure providers.

2.4 Payment information

Billing and payment card details are collected and processed directly by Paddle (paddle.com). We never store your card number, CVV, or full payment details.

2.5 Communications

If you contact us by email, we retain those communications to respond to you and improve our support.

3. Lawful Basis for Processing (GDPR / POPIA)

We process your personal information on the following lawful bases:

  • Contract performance — to provide the service you signed up for (account management, call answering, lead notifications, billing).
  • Legitimate interests — to send service-related emails (onboarding tips, usage alerts, trial expiry notices), to prevent fraud and abuse, and to improve our product. We have balanced these interests against your rights and determined they do not override them.
  • Legal obligation — to comply with applicable laws including tax, accounting, and data protection requirements.
  • Consent — for any marketing communications beyond service-related emails. You may withdraw consent at any time using the unsubscribe link in any email.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, operate, and improve the MissedCallio service.
  • To send you SMS notifications about leads captured during calls.
  • To send you transactional and service emails — including account verification codes, onboarding guidance (days 1, 3, and 6 after signup), usage alerts, trial expiry reminders, and subscription confirmations.
  • To process payments and manage your subscription through Paddle.
  • To respond to your support requests.
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling for marketing, or sale to third parties.

5. Automated Processing

Aria's AI conversations involve automated processing of caller speech to extract names, phone numbers, and service requests. This is not automated decision-making with legal or similarly significant effects on individuals — Aria captures information for a human (you, the business owner) to act on. You remain responsible for all decisions made based on information Aria captures.

6. Caller Data — Your Responsibilities as Data Controller

When Aria answers calls on behalf of your business, you are the data controller for the information your callers share. This means:

  • You are responsible for having a privacy notice that informs your callers that calls may be answered by an AI assistant, recorded, and transcribed.
  • Caller data is stored in your MissedCallio account, accessible only to you and authorised team members.
  • Caller data is never shared with other MissedCallio customers or sold to third parties.
  • You must comply with applicable data protection laws (POPIA, GDPR, CCPA, etc.) when storing, using, or sharing caller information.

8. International Data Transfers

MissedCallio operates globally. Most of our service providers are based in the United States. If you are located in the EU, UK, or South Africa, your personal data is transferred to the USA when processed by these providers. We rely on the following safeguards for these transfers:

  • Data Processing Agreements (DPAs) with all sub-processors that include standard contractual clauses (SCCs) approved by the relevant supervisory authority.
  • The providers listed above have independently certified data protection practices under applicable frameworks.

If you have questions about international transfers or wish to request copies of applicable safeguards, contact us at support@missedcallio.online.

9. Data Retention

  • While your account is active: we retain all account and call data.
  • After cancellation: your account data is retained for up to 30 days to allow for reactivation, after which it is deleted or anonymised unless we are required by law to retain it longer.
  • Upon deletion request: we will delete your personal data within 30 days of a verified request, except for data we are legally required to retain (e.g. billing records for tax purposes, which may be retained for up to 7 years).
  • Call recordings and transcripts: retained for the duration of your account and deleted with it.
  • Authentication OTPs and session tokens: expired tokens are purged within 24 hours of expiry.

10. Data Security

We take security seriously and implement technical and organisational measures including:

  • Data encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest via Supabase's infrastructure.
  • Authentication using short-lived OTP codes and signed session tokens — no passwords stored.
  • Row-level security controls limiting access to your data.
  • Webhook signature verification to prevent spoofed events.
  • Access to production systems restricted to authorised personnel only.

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. If we discover a security breach that is likely to affect your rights, we will notify you by email without undue delay and in any case within 72 hours of becoming aware of it.

11. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data. We will respond to all verified requests within 30 days.

For all users

  • Access: request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion ("right to be forgotten"): request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal retention obligations.
  • Data portability: receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format (CSV export available in your dashboard).
  • Opt-out of marketing: unsubscribe from non-essential emails using the link in any email we send.

For EU / UK users (GDPR / UK GDPR)

  • Object to processing: object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Restrict processing: request that we restrict processing of your data in certain circumstances.
  • Lodge a complaint: you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority (e.g. the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) in the UK, or the relevant EU data protection authority in your member state).

For California users (CCPA / CPRA)

  • Know: right to know what personal information we collect, use, disclose, and sell.
  • Delete: right to request deletion of your personal information.
  • Opt-out of sale: we do not sell personal information. If this changes, we will provide a "Do Not Sell My Personal Information" mechanism.
  • Non-discrimination: we will not discriminate against you for exercising your CCPA rights.
  • Correct: right to correct inaccurate personal information.

For South African users (POPIA)

  • You have the right to access, correct, and delete your personal information as provided under the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).
  • You may object to the processing of your personal information on reasonable grounds.
  • Complaints may be submitted to the Information Regulator of South Africa at justice.gov.za/inforeg.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@missedcallio.online. We may ask you to verify your identity before processing a request.

12. Cookies and Local Storage

Our website and dashboard use browser storage to function. See our Cookie Policy for the full list of what we store, why, and for how long. You can manage your cookie preferences via the consent banner on our homepage.

In brief: we use session tokens in localStorage to keep you logged in, and a consent flag to remember your cookie preference. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

13. Children's Privacy

MissedCallio is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a minor, contact us immediately and we will delete it.

14. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites. We encourage you to read their privacy policies before sharing any personal information with them.

15. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by email at least 14 days before they take effect. The current version is always available at missedcallio.online/privacy. Continued use of the service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

16. Contact Us

We aim to respond to all privacy requests within 5 business days and to resolve them within 30 days.
