Incident Response Plan

Version 1.0  |  Effective: 1 November 2025  |  Reviewed annually

This Incident Response Plan ("IRP") documents how MissedCallio detects, contains, investigates, and notifies affected parties in the event of a security breach or personal-data incident. It is shared publicly to demonstrate our commitment to transparency and to help our customers understand their rights and what to expect if an incident occurs.

⚠ To report a security vulnerability or suspected breach: security@missedcallio.online

1. Scope

This plan applies to any event that results in, or is suspected to result in, the unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction of:

  • Personal data processed by MissedCallio (caller information, business-owner accounts, call transcripts, appointment records)
  • Confidential business data (API keys, credentials, subscription details)
  • MissedCallio infrastructure (servers, databases, third-party integrations)

3. Response Phases

Phase 1 Detection & Identification

  • Automated alerts (Railway logs, Supabase audit events, anomaly detection) trigger initial triage
  • Any team member may raise an incident by emailing security@missedcallio.online
  • Incident lead assigned; unique incident ID created; timeline started
  • Determine: What data? How many individuals? Which systems? Is it ongoing?

Phase 2 Containment

  • Short-term: Isolate affected systems; revoke compromised credentials; disable affected integrations; block attacker IP ranges if identifiable
  • Long-term: Deploy patched system image; rotate all secrets; force-logout all active sessions; re-audit access control
  • Preserve logs and artefacts — do not wipe affected systems until forensic copy secured

Phase 3 Investigation & Eradication

  • Determine root cause, attack vector, and full scope of data exposure
  • Remove malware, backdoors, or unauthorised access points
  • Apply patches and configuration hardening
  • Verify eradication before returning systems to production

Individual notification:

  • Affected individuals notified without undue delay and no later than the applicable regulatory deadline
  • Notification includes: nature of breach, categories of data, likely consequences, measures taken, contact for questions
  • Notification sent by email to the registered account address; SMS where feasible and urgency warrants

Customer notification (B2B):

  • Business customers whose caller data may be affected are notified within 48 hours of confirmed P1/P2 incident
  • Notification via email to the business account owner; dashboard banner if systems are operational

Phase 5 Recovery

  • Restore systems from verified clean backups where necessary
  • Monitor closely for 72 hours post-restoration for reinfection or residual attacker access
  • Gradually restore service, verifying functionality and security at each step

Phase 6 Post-Incident Review

  • Post-mortem within 14 days of incident closure
  • Root cause analysis; timeline of events; what worked, what didn't
  • Remediation action items with owners and deadlines
  • Update this IRP and security controls based on lessons learned
  • Summary shared with affected customers on request

5. Responsible Disclosure

MissedCallio operates a responsible disclosure programme. If you discover a security vulnerability in our platform:

  1. Email a description of the vulnerability to security@missedcallio.online
  2. Allow us a reasonable time (typically 90 days) to investigate and patch before public disclosure
  3. Do not access, modify, or exfiltrate data beyond what is necessary to demonstrate the vulnerability
  4. Do not disrupt service availability or perform social engineering against our staff

We will acknowledge valid reports within 5 business days, keep you informed of remediation progress, and credit you publicly (with your permission) upon patch release.

6. Third-Party Incidents

MissedCallio relies on third-party processors including Vapi AI, Deepgram, Anthropic, ElevenLabs, Twilio, Supabase, Railway, Paddle, Brevo, and Resend. If any of these providers experiences a breach affecting MissedCallio customer data:

  • MissedCallio will assess the impact on our customers within 24 hours of learning of the provider incident
  • Affected customers will be notified with available details as soon as practicable
  • Mitigation steps (credential rotation, feature disablement) will be taken within the response SLA for the assessed severity
