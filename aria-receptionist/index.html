FAQ

Common questions

Does the caller know they're talking to an AI?

Aria introduces herself by name and acts naturally — she doesn't say "I'm an AI." Most callers assume she's a real receptionist. You can add a disclosure in your business settings if you prefer full transparency.

What happens when I'm available to answer myself?

Just don't forward calls to your MissedCallio number. Call forwarding is under your control — turn it on when you're busy or after hours, turn it off when you're free. Aria only answers the calls you send her.

Can I use my existing phone number?

Yes. You keep your existing number. Aria gets a dedicated number, and you set up call forwarding from your number to hers — typically a one-time setup that takes under 2 minutes.

What if I already have voicemail?

Aria is better than voicemail — she has a real conversation, captures the caller's name, number, and reason for calling, books appointments if needed, and sends you an SMS instantly. Most businesses disable voicemail once Aria is set up.

What industries does this work for?

Any business that takes phone calls: plumbers, electricians, lawyers, therapists, dentists, real estate agents, accountants, salons, gyms, contractors, and more. If missing a call means missing money, Aria helps.

How quickly do I get set up?

Signup takes under 3 minutes. You'll receive a dedicated phone number immediately. Call forwarding setup takes another 2 minutes. Most businesses have Aria live within 5 minutes of signing up.

Can I cancel at any time?

Yes — cancel anytime from your dashboard with no questions asked. You keep access until the end of your billing period. We also offer a full refund within 7 days if Aria isn't right for your business.
